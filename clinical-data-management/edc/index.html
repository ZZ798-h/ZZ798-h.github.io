Background

What Is EDC?

Electronic Data Capture

An Electronic Data Capture (EDC) system collects and manages clinical research data in structured electronic case report forms (eCRFs). Study teams use it to enter, review, clarify, and prepare data for downstream cleaning and analysis.

Why It Matters

The casebook structure, field definitions, required values, status controls, and audit history support consistent collection and traceable review. These controls directly affect the reliability of the final clinical dataset.

Process

Workflow Practiced

The evidence below follows the work from casebook orientation to reviewable output.

  1. Verify context Confirm the fictional subject, visit, form, and displayed source context.
  2. Complete eCRFs Enter and review required values across Screening and Baseline forms.
  3. Review status Check required fields, controlled selections, units, ranges, and form completion.
  4. Confirm sign-off Review the fictional investigator sign-off step and completion state.
  5. Inspect export Review the JSON export and audit records, then remove tokens before publication.

What This Practice Demonstrates

This case study demonstrates casebook navigation, structured eCRF entry, completion review, sign-off awareness, audit-record review, and export handling in a training environment. The completed run contained no queries, so I do not present query resolution as completed evidence here.

Reflection

Key Takeaways

  • Correct subject, visit, and form selection is a practical data-quality control.
  • eCRF design shapes completeness, consistency, units, and permissible values.
  • “Complete” status should be followed by source, logic, and cross-form review.
  • Audit context and safe export handling connect operational entry with QC and programming.
