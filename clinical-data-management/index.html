Clinical Data Management Learning Lab

A structured record of hands-on practice in EDC/eCRF workflows, data cleaning, query management, QC, traceability, and clinical data integrity.

This learning lab translates clinical data management concepts into small, reviewable portfolio artifacts. Each topic documents the task, the evidence produced, why the step matters, and what I learned from the practice.

View EDC & eCRF Practice Back to Portfolio

Portfolio Scope

Training evidence, not production-system experience

The materials in this lab use fictional or synthetic data in educational environments. No real patient information is used. The goal is to demonstrate careful workflow reasoning, documentation, and readiness to learn organization-specific systems.
